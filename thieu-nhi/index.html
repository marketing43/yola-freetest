Part 2 · Reading

Look, read and choose ✓ or ✗.

There is one example. Nhìn hình, đọc câu: câu đúng với hình chọn ✓, câu sai chọn ✗.

Part 3 · Reading

Read the text and choose the correct response.

There is one example. Đọc lời của Dan và chọn câu trả lời phù hợp của Nick.

Example

Dan: I think an amusement park is the best place for us last weekend.

Nick: A. Here you are!  ·  B. What a beautiful flower!  ·  C. So do I!

Part 4 · Reading

Read the story and choose the word.

There is one example. Đọc truyện, chọn từ đúng cho mỗi chỗ trống (1)–(3).

Emma and the tiger

Emma and her family lived in the village near the (0) jungle. Every morning, she climbed her favourite apple tree and picked some apples from the top of the tree for her family. One morning, she was sitting in the tree when she saw an (1) …………… tiger below her. ‘Go away!’ she shouted. ‘I can’t come down until you go!’ But the tiger didn’t go away. She was very (2) …………… when she heard the tiger answer. ‘I’m not dangerous. Climb down. Those mangoes look heavy. I’ll help you get home. You can ride on my back.’

Emma climbed down the tree (3) ……………. When she was on the ground again and looked into the tiger’s kind eyes, she didn’t feel frightened. It was fun to ride on the tiger’s back. She smiled and told her family about riding a tiger home. All members of her family didn’t believe her but she didn’t mind! She knew she had an exciting and really kind new friend, the tiger, and that made her very happy.
